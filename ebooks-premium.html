The problem

Nobody warns you about the planning stage.

You've decided to build, or finally fix up the home you live in. You start searching, and every answer contradicts the last. Contractors want a decision. Designers want a deposit. Everyone seems sure, and you're not.

So you move forward anyway. Months and a lot of money later, you find out what you should have known on day one.

Most costly home-project mistakes aren't made on site. They're made before the project starts.

Early decisions shape the total cost of a project. Mistakes found late are the most expensive to fix. The good news: the cheapest step in the whole project is reading before you commit.

Homeowners who are planning to build, renovate or upgrade, and want clarity before spending money on design or construction. The guides are written for homeowners and first-time builders, not professionals.

No. They are useful at any stage: before you commit to a project, while you compare options, or when you are planning upgrades to the home you already own.

If you are unsure where to begin, start with the short guide. If you are choosing between a new build and an upgrade, read the build-or-upgrade guide. If you already have a contractor in mind, read the contractor guide.
