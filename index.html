Why Do Custom House Plans Blow Budgets?

It’s not that the dream was too big. It’s that the steps happened in the wrong order. Most families commit to expensive custom drawings before they have any idea what the home will actually cost to build.

It’s like buying a car without ever asking the price.

The smarter way flips it around:

  1. Start with a proven, professionally designed home
  2. Get its estimation drawings for a fraction of the cost of custom plans
  3. Send them to builders and get real quotes
  4. Then decide, with real numbers in your hand
